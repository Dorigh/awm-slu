About the chapter

The SLU Student Chapter of AWM mentors and encourages women and other minorities as they prepare for careers in mathematical sciences. We encourage conversation in the mathematics community among women and other gender minorities — connecting undergraduate students, graduate students, and faculty at both formal talks and casual mathematics gatherings.

We welcome all students irrespective of gender, gender identity, or major. While we're a math club, we're not aimed only at math students — we welcome all neighboring fields and interests.
